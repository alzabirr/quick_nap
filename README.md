## 🎮 Play Wake Up Games (Beat Sleep Inertia)

Say goodbye to mindlessly pressing snooze. Quick Nap is a smart alarm clock with games built specifically for heavy sleepers. You must play and solve randomly selected retro mini-games to dismiss your alarm. By engaging your brain in light problem-solving, you naturally shake off sleepiness and eliminate sleep inertia.

* **Memory Match:** Find the pairs to wake up your memory.
* **Color Reaction:** Tap the matching color fast to test your reflexes.
* **Word Scramble:** Unscramble letters to activate your cognitive skills.
* **Simon Says:** Follow light patterns to boost mental alertness.
* **Other Games:** Bubble Pop, TicTacToe, Puzzle Slide, Typing Speed, and more!
